FACTS ABOUT ANIMALS
1. Elephants are the largest land animals in the world. They have long trunks that help them drink water, pick up food, and communicate.
2. Dolphins are intelligent marine mammals. They communicate using clicks and whistles and often live in groups called pods.
3. Lions are large wild cats that live in groups called prides. Female lions usually do most of the hunting.
4. Giraffes are the tallest land animals in the world. Their long necks help them reach leaves on tall trees.
5. Penguins are flightless birds that are excellent swimmers. Most penguin species live in the Southern Hemisphere.
6. Bees are important pollinators. They help plants reproduce by transferring pollen between flowers.
7. Octopuses have eight arms and are known for their intelligence. They can change their skin colour to blend into their surroundings.
8. Cheetahs are the fastest land animals. They can reach speeds of around 100 kilometres per hour in short bursts.
9. Sea turtles are reptiles that spend most of their lives in the ocean. Female sea turtles return to beaches to lay their eggs.
10. Orangutans are great apes found in the rainforests of Borneo and Sumatra. They spend much of their time in trees and primarily eat fruit.
